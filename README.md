# AgriQueue

AgriQueue is a smart procurement and mandi queue management platform designed for the **Smart India Hackathon 2026**.

It helps farmers book procurement slots, receive queue tokens, track their live mandi position, complete quality and weighbridge checks, and monitor procurement payments.

At the administrative level, the platform supports a nationwide hierarchy across:

**India → State → District → Procurement Centre**

with dedicated dashboards for officers, district admins, state admins, and national admins.

---

## Problem Statement

Agricultural procurement centres often face:

- Long farmer waiting times
- Unplanned mandi congestion
- Manual queue handling
- Lack of transparency in procurement status
- Delays in quality inspection and weighbridge processing
- Limited visibility into payment progress
- Difficulty monitoring procurement activity across multiple geographic levels

AgriQueue aims to digitize and streamline this entire process.
